YouTube media downloader

Queue one video or a playlist and extract audio to MP3 with FFmpeg.

HTTPS links only
Smaller file = faster transfer
Choose a quality. Direct stream is for one video; it starts the phone download while the server converts audio and does not save the completed MP3 on the server.

To download a whole playlist, paste its YouTube playlist URL once and choose Playlist. When it finishes, use Download all MP3s as ZIP—you do not need to tap every song. You can also select individual songs or select all completed MP3s for one combined ZIP. The ZIP groups songs into folders by source job so you can extract them into a folder on your phone.
